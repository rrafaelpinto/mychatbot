# Architecture Decision Record

Read related code, current ADRs under `docs/adr/`, and the decision owner's stated constraints. Create an ADR only after a decision is actually accepted.

Use the next numeric filename and include:

- title;
- status and date;
- context and forces that made a decision necessary;
- the decision, including its boundary;
- credible alternatives considered;
- consequences and trade-offs, including follow-up or reversal cost.

Record durable engineering reasoning, not meeting history, prompts, transcripts, implementation narration, or generic best practices. Link another ADR when superseding it rather than silently rewriting history. Keep private reflection in `.local/`.
